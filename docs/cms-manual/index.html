Getting Started CMS Manual 04 / 35
I.A. Logging In
Open the clinic's app URL on a tablet or desktop browser. The branded login screen is the entry point for all staff.
Step 1
Enter your staff username (e.g. admin, dentist, assistant, receptionist).
Step 2
Enter your password.
Step 3
Tick Remember me on clinic tablets to stay signed in between shifts.
Step 4
Click Log in to open the dashboard.
Note
Development accounts use the password password. In production, every staff member gets a personal account from the Administrator.
Getting Started CMS Manual 05 / 35
I.B. The Dashboard
The dashboard is your day-start screen. It summarizes what needs attention before you open any patient record.
Step 1
Review Today's appointments (pending and confirmed) to plan the day.
Step 2
Check Recent patients and Pending procedures (unsigned treatments, dentist view).
Step 3
Scan Follow-ups for confirmed appointments in the next 7 days.
Step 4
Use the Monthly statistics chart to see patient and appointment growth.
Tap any widget's View all link to drill into the full list.

Patients & Records CMS Manual 07 / 35
II.A. Finding a Patient
The patient registry lists everyone on record. Search works on name, patient number, or contact number as you type.
Step 1
Open Patients from the sidebar.
Step 2
Type in the Search box — results filter live.
Step 3
Tap the row to open the full patient record.
Note
Patient numbers follow the year format (e.g. 2026-0101) and are assigned automatically at registration.
Patients & Records CMS Manual 08 / 35
II.B. Registering a Patient
Registration captures the PDA patient information record. Only the required fields are mandatory; everything else can be added later.
Step 1
Click Register patient (or start a New intake from the sidebar for the full wizard).
Step 2
Fill Identity: first name, middle name (optional), last name, sex, birth date, civil status, nationality.
Step 3
Fill Contact (contact number and address required) and Emergency contact.
Step 4
Optionally complete the PDA additional details section (religion, nickname, phones, insurance, guardian for minors).
Step 5
Click Register patient to save. The patient number is assigned automatically.
You land on the new patient's record, ready for medical history and clinical data.
Patients & Records CMS Manual 09 / 35
II.C. The Patient Record
Every patient's digital chart lives on one page: identity, contact, medical history, consultations, and five clinical tabs.
Step 1
Read the header card: initials, age, sex, and civil status badges.
Step 2
Use Edit to update demographics (permission-based).
Step 3
Open the tabs: Appointments, Chart, Treatments, Files, and Consents.
Note
Only an Administrator can delete a record, and it is a soft delete — clinical history is retained until archival.
Patients & Records CMS Manual 10 / 35
II.D. Medical History
The pre-treatment screening mirrors PDA Page 1: ten yes/no questions plus the extended general-health, physician, dental history, women's health, and conditions checklist groups.
Step 1
Click Edit on the Medical history card.
Step 2
Answer each question with the No / Yes / N/A pills. A details box appears for every Yes.
Step 3
Expand the optional groups and tick the Medical conditions checklist chips that apply.
Step 4
Click Save. The display view shows badges for every answer.
Note
Medical history is only visible to the Dentist and Administrator roles.
Patients & Records CMS Manual 11 / 35
II.E. Consultations
Consultations capture the findings of each visit, including the PDA intraoral examination fields.
Step 1
In the Consultations card, click Add consultation.
Step 2
Enter the chief complaint (required) plus findings, diagnosis, treatment plan, and recommendations.
Step 3
Record the Intraoral examination: periodontal screening, occlusion class, overjet/overbite, appliances, and TMD findings.
Step 4
Tap Save consultation — it appears in the timeline, newest first.
Only the authoring dentist (or Administrator) can edit a consultation.

Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 13 / 35
III.A. The Dental Chart Tab
The Chart tab renders the patient's current dental state right on the record — no need to leave the page to see it.
Step 1
Open the patient record and tap the Chart tab.
Step 2
Read the colored twin-arch chart: each mark shows a condition or restoration per tooth.
Step 3
Tap Open chart editor to mark new findings on the full interactive chart page.
The tab shows the entry count so you can confirm the chart has data at a glance.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 14 / 35
III.B. Charting a Tooth
The chart uses a tool-first workflow: pick what you found, then tap where you found it. Every tap saves instantly and permanently.
Step 1
Tap a condition chip (✓ D M MO Im Sp Rf Un) or a restoration chip (Am Co JC Ab Att P In Imp S Rm).
Step 2
Tap the tooth for a whole-tooth finding, or one of its 5 surface zones (Occlusal, Mesial, Distal, Buccal, Lingual).
Step 3
The mark colors in immediately with a confirmation toast.
Note
Whole-tooth-only conditions (M, MO, Im, Sp, Un) disable the surface zones automatically. Switch Adult / Primary for children's charts.
The chart never erases — corrections are new entries, and the full history stays available.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 15 / 35
III.C. Chart History
Every chart entry is logged with the date and the clinician who recorded it. Tap a date to render the chart as it looked that day.
Step 1
Click History on the chart page to open the entry log.
Step 2
Tap a dated entry to view the chart state as of that date.
Step 3
Use Back to current to return to today's view.
This is the audit trail — ideal for showing treatment progress at review visits.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 16 / 35
III.D. Treatment Records
The Treatments tab lists procedures performed for the patient, with a Signed or Pending badge per record.
Step 1
Open Treatments and tap Add treatment.
Step 2
Pick the date, tooth (or Non-tooth procedure), and procedure from the suggestions or type your own.
Step 3
Optionally link the related consultation and add a description.
Step 4
Tap Save treatment — it appears with a Pending badge until signed.
Note
Tap a row to expand details, including the embedded signature once signed.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 17 / 35
III.E. Signing a Treatment
A treatment record is complete once the dentist signs it. Signing captures a vector signature, the signer, and the timestamp.
Step 1
Open the Treatments tab and expand a Pending record.
Step 2
Tap Sign treatment to open the signature pad.
Note
Only the dentist who created the treatment (or the Administrator) can sign it.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 18 / 35
III.F. The Signature Pad
Draw your signature with a finger or stylus. The pad stores a crisp vector SVG — infinitely zoomable for printing.
Step 1
Draw your signature on the pad.
Step 2
Tap Clear to start over if needed.
Step 3
Tap Accept signature to lock it in — the badge flips to Signed.
Note
The accept button stays disabled until the pad has ink, preventing accidental empty signatures.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 19 / 35
III.G. Consents on the Record
The Consents tab lists every waiver form for the patient with its signing status.
Step 1
Open the Consents tab to see each form, its version, and status badge.
Step 2
Tap View to open the printable form.
Step 3
For a Patient signed form, tap Dentist sign to countersign with the signature pad.
Fully signed forms show the Signed badge; superseded drafts are kept as Voided for the audit trail.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 20 / 35
III.H. The Printable Consent Form
The signed form is an A4-ready legal document: all ten PDA statements with initials, both signatures, names, timestamps, and device info.
Step 1
Open the form via View on the Consents tab.
Step 2
Scroll through the ten sections, acknowledgment, authorization, and signature blocks.
Step 3
Click Print to send it to a printer or save as PDF.
Note
The footer records the IP address and device — part of the medico-legal evidence set.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 21 / 35
III.I. Patient Files
The Files tab stores X-rays, images, PDFs, prescriptions, and lab results per patient, filterable by category.
Step 1
Open the Files tab and tap Upload file.
Step 2
Pick a category; for X-rays also pick the X-ray type (Periapical, Panoramic, Cephalometric, Occlusal).
Step 3
Choose the file (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, or PDF) and watch the progress bar.
Note
Accepted files are limited to 25 MB by default — the limit is configurable in Settings.
Clinical Charting & Care CMS Manual 22 / 35
III.J. Viewing a File
Tap any thumbnail or tile to open the full-screen viewer — ideal for inspecting X-rays.
Step 1
Tap an image or X-ray thumbnail.
Step 2
Use the zoom buttons to inspect details (0.5× to 3×).
Step 3
PDFs open in an inline viewer; press Esc or tap outside to close.
Note
Only an Administrator can delete attachments, and deletions are soft (audited) for medico-legal reasons.

Intake Wizard
The 7-step guided patient intake, saved at every step
📋
Intake Wizard CMS Manual 24 / 35
IV.A. Starting Intake
The wizard registers a walk-in patient and completes the whole intake in one sitting — Patient, Medical history, Waiver, Signature, Consultation, Dental chart, Treatment.
Step 1
Click New intake in the sidebar.
Step 2
Fill in the patient form (identity, contact, emergency, optional PDA details).
Step 3
Tap Register & continue — the patient number is assigned automatically.
Note
Every step saves independently. If the tablet dies mid-intake, re-open the wizard and it resumes at the first incomplete step.
Intake Wizard CMS Manual 25 / 35
IV.B. Medical History Step
Step 2 captures the full PDA screening: ten questions plus the extended groups and 36-condition checklist.
Step 1
Answer the ten questions with No / Yes / N/A pills; detail boxes appear on Yes.
Step 2
Open the optional groups (General health, Physician, Dental history, Women's health, Conditions checklist).
Step 3
Tap Save & continue to move to the waiver.
The stepper shows completed steps with a check — you can jump back anytime.
Intake Wizard CMS Manual 26 / 35
IV.C. Waiver & Initials
Hand the tablet to the patient. Step 3 shows the ten PDA consent statements, each requiring the patient's initial on its own pad.
Step 1
Read each statement and draw the patient's initials in the box beside it.
Step 2
Review the acknowledgment and authorization blocks.
Step 3
Tap Save & continue — it stays disabled until all 10 sections are initialed.
Note
For patients under 18, a guardian signature is required on the next step.
Intake Wizard CMS Manual 27 / 35
IV.D. Patient Signature
Step 4 captures the patient's full signature on the consent form. For minors, a guardian name and signature pad appear automatically.
Step 1
Draw the patient's signature and tap Accept signature.
Step 2
If the patient is under 18, confirm the guardian name and capture the guardian's signature.
Step 3
Tap Sign & continue — the form is stored with names, timestamps, and device info.
The wizard then continues to the consultation, dental chart, and treatment steps.

Appointments & Administration CMS Manual 29 / 35
V.A. The Day View
Appointments open on a one-day view — the tablet-friendly schedule. Each row shows the time, patient, reason, dentist, and status.
Step 1
Use the ← / → chevrons to change days, or Today to jump back.
Step 2
Read the status badges: Pending, Confirmed, Completed, No-show, Cancelled.
Step 3
Tap New appointment to schedule.
Note
Overlapping appointments for the same dentist are rejected automatically unless overlapping is allowed in Settings.
Appointments & Administration CMS Manual 30 / 35
V.B. Creating an Appointment
The create dialog keeps scheduling to one screen: search the patient, pick the dentist, set the time window.
Step 1
Tap New appointment.
Step 2
Type to search the patient by name or patient number, then tap the result.
Step 3
Optionally assign a dentist, set the date and time window, and add a reason.
Step 4
Tap Create appointment — it appears as Pending.
Useful reasons like "Follow-up after composite restoration" feed the dashboard's Follow-ups widget.
Appointments & Administration CMS Manual 31 / 35
V.C. Appointment Actions
Tapping an appointment opens its detail sheet with state-aware actions — the buttons shown depend on the current status.
Step 1
Tap an appointment row to open the detail sheet.
Step 2
Confirm a pending appointment to lock the schedule.
Step 3
On the day: Mark attended or Mark no-show.
Step 4
Reschedule moves it back to Pending; Cancel requires a reason.
Note
Cancelled, completed, and no-show appointments are closed — no further actions apply.
Appointments & Administration CMS Manual 32 / 35
V.D. Managing Users
Users is Administrator-only. Staff accounts are created with a username, email, password, and role.
Step 1
Open Users from the sidebar to see the staff list with roles and status.
Step 2
Use Edit to change details or deactivate a departing staff member's account.
Note
Roles control what each person sees: Administrator, Dentist, Assistant, Receptionist. You cannot deactivate your own account.
Appointments & Administration CMS Manual 33 / 35
V.E. Adding a User
New staff get a username (their login), an email (for system mail), a password, and one of the four roles.
Step 1
Click Add user.
Step 2
Enter name, username (unique — used to sign in), email, and an initial password.
Step 3
Select the role and save.
Note
A welcome email is queued automatically; it reads the clinic name from Settings.
Appointments & Administration CMS Manual 34 / 35
V.F. Clinic Settings
Settings holds clinic-wide values grouped by area. Changes apply immediately — including the landing page brand and upload limits.
Step 1
Open Settings (Administrator only).
Step 2
Edit any group: General, Scheduling, Patient records, Consent, Uploads, Archiving.
Step 3
Tap Save settings — a toast confirms the update.
Note
The Archiving group sets how many years of inactivity trigger patient archival (default 5).
Appointments & Administration CMS Manual 35 / 35
V.G. Reports
Reports summarizes the whole clinic over a date range — growth, attendance, procedures, chart conditions, and dentist workload.
Step 1
Open Reports (Administrator only) and set the From / To dates.
Step 2
Read the metric cards: new patients, appointments, procedures, average attendance.
Step 3
Review the five charts: patient growth, appointment summary, procedures, conditions, dentist workload.
Step 4
Click CSV to export any chart's data, or Print for a PDF copy.
Note
Condition summary doubles as a clinical audit — spot frequently recorded conditions early.
